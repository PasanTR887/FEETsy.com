FAQ's



How can i place the order ?

To place an order, simply browse through our wide selection of shoes and fashionable footwear on our website. Once you’ve found the items you love, select your size and quantity, and then proceed to checkout. Follow the prompts to enter your shipping and payment details, and your order will be on its way!

What payment methods do you accept ?

We accept a variety of payment methods, including credit/debit cards, PayPal, and other secure online payment options. Rest assured that your payment information is safe and secure with us.

How long will it take for my order to arrive ?

Shipping times may vary depending on your location and the shipping method chosen at checkout. Generally, orders within Colombo are delivered within 2-3 business days, while orders outside Colombo may take 5-7 business days.

Do you offer international shipping ?

Currently, we only offer shipping within Sri Lanka. We do not provide international shipping services at this time.

What if i receive the wrong item or a defective product ?

In the unlikely event that you receive the wrong item or a defective product, please contact our customer service team immediately. We will gladly assist you with a replacement or refund as per our Returns and Refunds policy.

If you have any other questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out to us. Our dedicated team is here to help!
